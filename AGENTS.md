# Zartema engineering instructions

- Read `MISSION.md` and `PROJECT_STATE.md` before substantial work. Preserve the full mission across sessions and do not equate a milestone with completion.
- Use the installed OBA V3 `oba-workflow` and applicable domain skills. Read the relevant technical sections before consequential decisions, record actual evidence and distinguish specialist reports from direct reads.
- Preserve authorized, legitimate use; exclude security-tool evasion, malicious concealment, anti-analysis mechanisms and unauthorized third-party protection bypass.
- Keep class-file emission, analysis snapshot ownership, uncertainty, pass assumptions, compatibility limits and failure behavior explicit. Never silently weaken a protection or suppress verification failures.
- Test changed behavior with relevant structural checks, actual target-JVM execution and bounded original-versus-transformed comparisons. A verifier or green build is not semantic proof.
- Maintain current state, evidence and ADRs. Keep source, documentation, diagnostics and commits in English and project metadata attributed to `estacyl`.
- Do not commit credentials, local caches, unrelated files or the installed OBA knowledge base. Preserve third-party licenses. Coordinate edits with other contributors and do not revert their work.
